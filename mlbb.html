ABOUT THE EVENT

MLBB Campus League

About

MLBB Campus League is a competitive Mobile Legends tournament for student teams. Eight teams compete in exciting 5v5 matches across three competition days.

Purpose

The tournament promotes teamwork, communication, strategic thinking and healthy competition while giving students an opportunity to experience an organised e-sports event.

Format

Eight teams compete in a single-elimination bracket. Quarter Finals and Semi Finals are played as Best of 3, while the Grand Final is played as Best of 5.
