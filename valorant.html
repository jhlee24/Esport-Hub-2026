ABOUT THE EVENT

Valorant Summer Cup

About

Valorant Summer Cup is a campus e-sports tournament for competitive Valorant teams. Eight teams compete in tactical 5v5 matches across three competition days.

Purpose

The tournament promotes teamwork, communication, strategic thinking and healthy competition while giving students an opportunity to experience a structured e-sports competition.

Format

Eight teams compete in a single-elimination bracket. Quarter Finals and Semi Finals are played as Best of 3, while the Grand Final is played as Best of 5.
