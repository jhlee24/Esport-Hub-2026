ABOUT THE EVENT

Apex Legends Showdown

About

Apex Legends Showdown is a campus e-sports tournament where squads compete in fast-paced battle royale matches to earn points and reach the final stage.

Purpose

The event promotes teamwork, communication, quick thinking and competitive strategy among student players.

Format

Eight squads compete in a 3-player format. Teams progress through elimination rounds before the Grand Final.
