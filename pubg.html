ABOUT THE EVENT

PUBG Battle Arena

About

PUBG Battle Arena is a competitive battle royale tournament for student squads. Eight squads compete across three competition days using teamwork, positioning and tactical decision-making.

Purpose

The tournament promotes teamwork, communication, map awareness and strategic decision-making while giving students an opportunity to experience an organised e-sports competition.

Format

Eight squads compete through a three-day elimination format. Quarter Finals and Semi Finals use Best of 3 rounds, while the Grand Final uses Best of 5 to decide the champion.
