ABOUT THE EVENT

Rocket League Championship

About

Rocket League Championship is a competitive campus tournament where teams use powered vehicles to score goals in exciting high-speed matches.

Purpose

The tournament encourages teamwork, communication, quick decision-making and competitive spirit among students.

Format

Eight teams compete in a 3v3 single-elimination format. Quarter Finals and Semi Finals are Best of 5, while the Grand Final is Best of 7.
